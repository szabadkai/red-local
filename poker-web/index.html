How do I draw cards?

You start with five cards. Every three sword swings that hit an enemy drop one card. Touch it to pause the action and reveal two choices. Click a card or press 1 / 2 to take it; the other is discarded and play resumes. Esc leaves the pickup untouched: step away before touching it again. With a full hand, your chosen card is saved. Click a hand card or press 1–5 to replace it, or play with K to fill an empty slot with the saved card. Getting hit never takes your cards.

Pairs hit hard, two pair explodes, trips and straights pierce. Flushes and higher transform you into Dark Red for six seconds. Collect three embers in each clearing and reach its lantern. Each lantern leads straight to the next clearing with full health and a fresh hand. Runners lunge, mages commit to an aimed shot you can dodge or slash, and guards telegraph a heavy slam. New journey starts from Lantern Wood. Progress saves at clearing entrances in this browser; Continue deals a fresh hand with full health. Practice is separate from your saved journey.
